The client experience

By Other Construction is a family-owned, veteran-led general contractor that has served Central Florida since 2008 — residential and commercial alike. We bring over 40 years of combined industry experience to every project, from a homeowner's remodel to a full tenant build-out.

Communication and transparency come first: you always know the schedule, the cost and who to call. That's how we've earned repeat work from homeowners and national retail and restaurant brands alike.

Frequently asked questions

What services do you provide?

Framing, interior buildouts & remodels, drywall installation & finishing, demolition, plumbing, painting and concrete — for residential and commercial projects.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. GC License # CBC1265846, fully insured, family-owned and veteran-led since 2008.

Do you help with design or getting plans drawn up?

Yes — we can help you develop or refine plans and specs as part of a design-build approach, so your project is properly scoped before we ever start building.

What should I plan for before a buildout or remodel?

Layout, budget, permits and (for commercial spaces) ADA compliance. We guide you through design, permits, construction and finishing.

What does a demolition job include?

Site assessment, safe dismantling, debris removal, site cleanup and permits — from a single room to full building removal.

Where do you work?

We work throughout Central Florida, on residential and commercial projects alike — contact us with your location and we'll confirm.

How long has By Other Construction been in business?

Since 2008 — over 40 years of combined industry experience across our team.
