The client experience

By Other Construction is a family-owned, veteran-led general contractor that has served Central Florida businesses since 2008. We bring over 40 years of combined industry experience to every project, from a single tenant build-out to a full commercial renovation.

Communication and transparency come first: you always know the schedule, the cost and who to call. That's how we've earned repeat work from national retail and restaurant brands.

Areas of expertise

We specialize in commercial construction for retail, restaurant and office spaces: framing, interior build-outs, drywall, demolition and plumbing. Tell us what you're building and we'll tell you plainly how we'd build it, what it costs and how long it takes.

Frequently asked questions

What services do you provide?

Commercial framing, interior buildouts, drywall installation & finishing, demolition and plumbing — for retail, restaurant and office spaces.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. GC License # CBC1265846, fully insured, family-owned and veteran-led since 2008.

What should I plan for before a buildout?

Layout, budget, permits and ADA compliance. We guide you through design, permits, construction and finishing.

Site assessment, safe dismantling, debris removal, site cleanup and permits — from interior strip-outs to full building removal.
